# Ashfall Chronicles v1.1.4 — Deeper Down, Further Out

**Saves from v1.1.x open as they are.** Ruins, depth layers, caves, sky islands and the floating citadel are generated
differently now, so those changes appear in newly created worlds; an existing world keeps its terrain.

Downloads are the same as v1.1.3:

| Edition | Files | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Browser edition | `AshfallChronicles-1.1.4-Windows.zip` · `-macOS.zip` | Small. A launcher opens the game in your default browser. |
| App edition | `AshfallChronicles-1.1.4-App-Windows.zip` · `-App-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip` · `-App-macOS-Intel.zip` · `-App-Linux.zip` | About 200 MB. Opens in its own window (Electron). |

Neither edition is signed with a developer certificate, so your OS asks once on first launch.
**Windows** — *More info → Run anyway*. **macOS 14/15** — click **Done**, then *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*.
Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `SHA256SUMS-App.txt`.

## New

- **Rides.** Buy an ash-maned horse from Rika the tamer, build a wooden skiff, or lay rails and ride a mine cart.
  The horse runs about 2.5× faster and leaps higher; the two-tile skiff glides over fresh water and the sea without using breath
  (a cargo skiff with a lashed crate can be built too — right-click while aboard to open its 16-slot hold);
  the cart follows rails, picks up speed on slopes and stops at the bumper. Rides stay where you get off and are saved with the world.
- **Fishing duel.** When a fish bites, an osu!-style duel opens: hit circles on the beat, follow slider balls, spin spinners.
  The gauge fills to land the catch and drains if you miss. What bit the hook decides the duel — rare catches are faster,
  smaller and denser, and may spin the field, hide approach circles, wiggle or pull your cursor. Rod, bait and fishing
  mastery make the duel easier. Prefer the old one-click fishing? Turn off *Fishing duel* in Settings.
- **Boss openings.** Every boss now has signature moves with a warning, a brace while it gathers power (hits do less), and an opening
  afterwards — staggered, overheated or cracked armour — where hits land harder. Make it miss and the opening lasts longer.
- **Ruins rebuilt.** Every ruin has its own bricks, decor, layout tendency, pulse events and a monster that fights by its own rules.
  Sealed rooms lock you in until you solve their puzzle; solving them all opens deeper floors with themed keys.
  Some ruins now lie at hell depth or are buried with no surface entrance. Story ruins hide three memory shards each.
- **Floating citadel.** Align three orbital anchors to raise light bridges and open the sealed gate; the vault inside holds a jetpack prototype.
- **Depth and caves.** New depth layers between the underground and hell (deepslate, then basalt with magma veins), plus big caverns
  with lakes and waterfalls, long tunnels and crossing tunnels with camp chests.
- **Altitude.** Altitude is now measured from sea level (0 m) and shown signed; sky islands sit higher and no longer shade the ground.
- **Livelier windows.** Crafting materials fly into the result and the finished item flies to your bag; the anvil plays three hammer
  beats with a success / fail / break odds bar and distinct endings; machine slots bump as items arrive and progress bars shimmer.
- **Skills feel like events.** Screen flashes, focus lines, orbiting auras and per-class art; sword slashes vary by direction and combo.
- **Bosses with more to them.** Twelve smaller bosses are redrawn at 2–3× size with a pose for every move; each boss gains moves
  as its phases turn, and some heal, raise a shield (shown in blue on the health bar), fly up or split in two.
- **Monster signatures.** One monster per biome hunts with a move of its own, drawn from the animal it is based on.
- **Talent tree.** One web around a central heart instead of separate fields; nodes stay hidden until a neighbour is learned,
  you hold to learn, and new nodes unlock extra accessory, pet and bag slots. A new Explorer branch joins it.
- **Wild horses.** Horses roam the ash forest; feed them crops to raise their trust and tame one to ride.
- **Junk becomes useful.** Junk recipes make six odd weapons (shock claw, boomerang, leaf sword, frying pan, umbrella, bubble wand),
  recall orbs back to camp or the village, a retracing compass and a stopped hourglass.
- **Factories.** Multi-tile machines with animated bodies, a cutaway view of each machine at work, a ratio converter and IF/ELSE and OR
  splitters; machines rarely break down while working (they shake under a warning sign) and a craftable wrench repairs them.
- **Detectors.** Hold right-click to scan; ore and monster scans now have their own effects — tremor rings and light pillars over veins,
  a heartbeat ring and lock-on marks on monsters, arrows at the screen edge for those off-screen.
- **Chat and emojis.** A reworked multiplayer chat with 28 pixel emojis, many animated, including an Ashfall set.
- **New soundtrack.** All background music is new, with several tracks for bosses and the village.

## Changed

- HP and mana start refilling 5 seconds after you take damage or spend mana, and refill from empty in 250 seconds.
- The jetpack has no height limit any more: each burn lasts 15 seconds, and one battery cell powers 5 minutes of flight.
- During a fishing duel (solo) the world pauses, the camera closes in on the angler and the surroundings dim.
- About 40% of every ruin chest is now that ruin's own loot — its unique material, bricks, decor and the drops of its monsters.
- Early-game difficulty ramps more gently; monster HP and damage were rebalanced per session.
- Graves settle on the ground with new art; hit flashes follow the monster's silhouette.
- Typing in text fields no longer triggers game hotkeys.
- The fishing duel is gentler for beginners: slower beats, larger circles, a longer approach and a softer drain; the top tier is a mid-level osu! map.
- Damage over time and regeneration tick once a second with numbers; critical numbers stand out.
- Shadows fall on the ground — the player, trees, leaves and flowers cast them along the sun.
- The game font is an original Ashfall typeface, with rebuilt Hangul composition.
- Parallax backgrounds are rebaked in one style; dialogue appears in speech bubbles; drills look like real mining rigs.
- The floating citadel is a full castle on its island, reached over a bridge.
