# Ashfall Chronicles v1.1.2 — Walking Together

**Saves from v1.1.x open as they are.** v1.0.x saves still do not open (the world has been 5000 tiles wide since v1.1).

There are now **two downloads**:

| Edition | Files | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Browser edition | `AshfallChronicles-1.1.2-Windows.zip` · `-macOS.zip` | Small. A launcher opens the game in your default browser. |
| App edition | `AshfallChronicles-1.1.2-App-Windows.zip` · `-App-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip` · `-App-macOS-Intel.zip` · `-App-Linux.zip` | About 200 MB. Opens in its own window (Electron). |

Neither edition is signed with a developer certificate, so your OS asks once on first launch:
**Windows** — *More info → Run anyway*. **macOS 14/15** — open once, then *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*
(macOS 13 and earlier: right-click → Open). The macOS apps are ad-hoc signed, so macOS no longer calls them "damaged" or
pushes them to the Trash. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `SHA256SUMS-App.txt`.

## New

- **Multiplayer (2–4 players).** Open a room on one of your worlds and share the six-character code; friends join the host's world over
  a direct browser-to-browser connection. Characters are each player's own and are remembered by that world, so a guest picks up where
  they left off. The host can toggle chat and PvP, sees a party list with HP and latency, and can remove players.
- **Monsters use skills.** Some heal or empower nearby allies; others fire fire, frost and poison bolts or cast a curse. Casting is
  telegraphed (the body lifts and leans back while light gathers in its hand), so you can step out of the way.
- **Status effects you can see.** Burning bodies catch fire, chilled ones frost over, poisoned ones bubble. On you, frostbite, burn, poison and
  weakness also tint the screen edge — one layer per kind, so repeats never stack.
- **The Ashfall font.** A modified Pretendard (SIL Open Font License 1.1) used across all six languages.
- **Light and shadow.** Shadows lean away from the sun as it crosses the sky, torches no longer leak light through walls, and leaves and
  sky islands cast softer shade.
- **Ice caves** under the frost and glacier regions, with icicles that fall when you mine what holds them up; glowing fronds in mossy caves;
  hanging and standing decorations now collapse when their support is gone.
- **The ash lifts.** Finishing session 1 brings the ash forest's leaves and backdrop back.
- **New look.** Redrawn skill effects, a new title logo and screen, a new tab icon, redrawn village portraits, livelier pet idles and attacks,
  and dragons that visibly change at each stage.

- The blood-moon sky not showing over the base camp and the village.
- Picking up your own grave marker on the frame you died.
- Returning multiplayer guests being dropped next to the host when their last spot had been built over.
- Too many shadows on the ground from sky islands and tree leaves.
