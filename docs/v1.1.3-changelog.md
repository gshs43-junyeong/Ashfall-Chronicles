# Ashfall Chronicles v1.1.3 — Catching Fire

**Saves from v1.1.x open as they are.** Sky islands and trees are placed a little differently, so only newly created worlds change.

Downloads are the same as v1.1.2:

| Edition | Files | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Browser edition | `AshfallChronicles-1.1.3-Windows.zip` · `-macOS.zip` | Small. A launcher opens the game in your default browser. |
| App edition | `AshfallChronicles-1.1.3-App-Windows.zip` · `-App-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip` · `-App-macOS-Intel.zip` · `-App-Linux.zip` | About 200 MB. Opens in its own window (Electron). |

Neither edition is signed with a developer certificate, so your OS asks once on first launch.
**Windows** — *More info → Run anyway*. **macOS 14/15** — the first launch shows "Apple could not verify … is free of malware".
Click **Done** (*Move to Trash* deletes the app), then *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*. macOS shows this for every app
that is not notarized through a paid Apple account. To skip it, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Ashfall Chronicles.app"`.
Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `SHA256SUMS-App.txt`.

## New

- **Redrawn skill effects.** Slashes, shockwaves, flashes, rune-carved magic circles, light pillars, beams and smoke are now drawn
  from baked textures instead of plain circles and lines, tinted to each element.
- **Monsters catch fire.** A burning monster glows hot inside its outline while flames climb its body. Frozen monsters frost over
  along their outline instead of inside a square box.
- **Flying monsters.** Bats, wraiths and anything in mid-air no longer get a ground line drawn in the air — flames, frost, casting
  glow and empower auras follow the body.
- **Hopping rabbits.** Rabbits and arctic hares hop instead of bobbing in place.
- **World.** No sky islands are generated over the base camp or Dawnhold, and trees near extraction rigs are no longer left
  with clipped canopies or floating leaves.
- **New look.** A slimmer title logo (the sky arch is gone), the app now uses the new tab icon, and the download page has step-by-step
  instructions for the app edition.

## Fixed

- Monsters hit by skill fire (meteor shower and the like) took damage but never looked like they were burning.
- The app edition shipped with the old icon.
